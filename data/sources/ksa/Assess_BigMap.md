## Phase 3 - Skill Scenario Questions

### S.1 [digital_craft]
A software UI is confusing users. What do you do?
- A: Add more text instructions.
- B: Simplify the layout and user flow.

### S.2 [operational_skills]
A process step keeps causing a bottleneck.
- A: Tell people to work faster.
- B: Redesign the workflow to balance the load.

### S.3 [strategic_execution]
You have a high-impact but high-risk idea.
- A: Avoid it to be safe.
- B: Create a mitigation plan and test a pilot.

### S.4 [relational_skills]
A client is angry about a late delivery.
- A: Explain why it’s not your fault.
- B: Acknowledge the frustration and offer a solution.

### S.5 [research_inquiry]
You find data that contradicts your theory.
- A: Ignore it as an outlier.
- B: Re-examine your theory based on the data.

### S.6 [literacy_numeracy]
You need to summarize a 50-page technical report.
- A: Read every word and rewrite it.
- B: Extract key findings and actionable insights.

## Phase 4 - Ability Questions

Time target per item: `30` seconds

### A.1 [quantitative_reasoning]
If 5 shirts cost $45, how much do 3 cost?
Expected answer reference: $27
Open-ended: False

### A.2 [verbal_comprehension]
Complete the analogy: ‘Oven’ is to ‘Heat’ as ‘Camera’ is to…
Expected answer reference: Light
Open-ended: False

### A.3 [spatial_visualization]
Mental Rotation: Can two ‘L’ shapes form a rectangle?
Expected answer reference: Yes
Open-ended: False

### A.4 [executive_function]
Identify the ‘odd one out’ in 5 seconds: 66, 88, 77, 98, 44.
Expected answer reference: 98
Open-ended: False

### A.5 [sensory_perceptual]
Listen to three tones: High, Mid, Low. Which was second?
Expected answer reference: Mid
Open-ended: False

### A.6 [social_emotional_capacity]
Someone looks away while you talk. They are likely…
Expected answer reference: Disinterested/Uncomfortable
Open-ended: False

### A.7 [divergent_thinking]
Name 3 uses for a coffee mug other than drinking.
Expected answer reference: open-ended
Open-ended: True
